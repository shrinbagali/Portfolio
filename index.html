About me

I'm a curious, fairly stubborn student of Computer Science with a focus on AI & ML at BLDEA CET, Vijayapura. Most of what I know, I picked up by trying to build something and getting stuck halfway through — then figuring out why. That process is what actually pulled me toward artificial intelligence and machine learning in the first place: the idea that you can teach a system to notice patterns you'd otherwise miss.

Outside of coursework, I spend a lot of time on hackathons, small AI/ML experiments, and web projects that started as "let me just try this." I also help run things on the organizing side as Secretary of my college's IEEE Student Branch, which has taught me almost as much about people and planning as my degree has taught me about code.
